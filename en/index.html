Privacy policy

G Navigation for Android - version 16, last updated: 31 October 2026.

G Navigation is a navigation app for motorbikes and cars in Vietnam that sends turn directions, maps and calls to your watch. It is developed and run by G Navigation (“we”). This page explains what data the app collects, what it is used for, how long it is kept, who can see it and how you control it. This policy follows Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP.

The short version: the app has no ads, uses no advertising identifier, and does not sell or share your data for marketing. Our servers keep no movement history linked to you. Notifications, contacts, calls, the microphone and the camera are used only when you turn on the feature that needs them.

1. What we collect and why

Account

You sign in with Google to navigate, use Premium and send contributions. We receive the account ID, display name and email address Google sends; we never receive your password. Each account is used on one phone at a time.

Location

Location is sensitive personal data under Decree 356/2025/ND-CP. The app asks for precise or approximate location and uses it in two ways; you can turn all of it off in Android settings:

  • On the device: to show where you are on the map, compare your speed with the limit and guide you turn by turn. While you navigate, the app keeps reading your location in the background so directions are not lost when the screen is off; background location is optional and asked separately.
  • Sent to the server per request: the map area in view, to return the map, signs, cameras and speed limits around it; your start, destination and vehicle, to plan a route; your location and heading, to reroute when you leave the route; your current location with what you type, to rank search results by distance. These requests are answered, and that is all; the server keeps no movement history linked to you. Location and speed during a trip, without your account, are also used to improve routing and arrival times.

Voice search

When you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text on the phone; only if you pick an online recogniser is the audio sent to it, under its own policy. We receive only the resulting text, never a recording, and keep no recording. The app listens only while the microphone is open.

Community reports

The signs, cameras and incidents you send are stored with their location and time of sending, and any photo or note, for us to moderate and show to other riders; photos have the information embedded in the file, such as where they were taken, removed. The app sends only what you save or report yourself; sending comes with this policy, which you accept when you install or update the app, with no separate switch. By submitting, you allow G Navigation to store, check, edit and share the road-sign content to provide and improve the map data.

Google Maps

On the Traffic and incidents screen only, the Google Maps SDK shows satellite imagery, the map and traffic with users' incident reports; Google Maps receives your IP address, device data and map gestures. Places picked there are routed on the app's map.

Watches and calls

Directions, the map and caller names go to the watch you paired over Bluetooth and the watch maker's app, not through our servers. These features are off until you turn them on:

  • Notification access: reads Google Maps guidance, and caller names and call state from Zalo, Messenger, Telegram, Viber, Instagram or LINE, to show them on the watch. Unrelated notifications are ignored; message contents are not stored or uploaded.
  • Contacts and Phone: creates temporary contacts carrying the caller's name so the watch shows it, and knows when a call is ringing. The app uploads no contacts, places no calls, and never listens to or records calls.
  • Voice replies: speech from the watch's microphone is recognised on the phone (or by an online recogniser if you pick one) and the reply is sent through the messaging app. Nothing is recorded.
  • Camera: only when you photograph a sign or an incident.

The G Navigation app for Wear OS on the watch uses notifications, full-screen, vibration, network and (optionally) the Phone permission to answer calls on the watch; it does not use the watch's location, microphone or contacts.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We receive only the product ID and purchase token to grant and restore Premium, never card details.

Technical data

  • Abuse prevention: the number of search and route requests per account (counted with a hash, not the account ID), and the IP address at the network service in front of our servers. Used to limit request rates and temporarily block accounts that scrape data automatically.
  • Device attestation: a check that requests come from the official app, with a hashed device identifier to stop repeated trials. We receive only a valid / not valid result.
  • Notifications: an installation ID and messaging token of the platform's push notification service, to deliver update and service notices.
  • Update checks: the app asks the G Navigation server about new versions of the app and the watch apps; the requests carry no account.
  • Error reports: technical logs stay on the phone; a report is sent only when you tap Send and confirm, and the app never sends one by itself.
  • Guidance voice: the G Navigation voice speaks directions on the phone, after its voice package is downloaded once. If you pick an online system voice, guidance sentences may be sent to that voice's provider.

2. What we do not collect

  • SMS messages, the call log, calendars, photos, files or other content on your device. The app never listens to or records calls.
  • Advertising identifiers, tracking cookies, data from data brokers.
  • Payment card details - payments are handled by Google Play.

5. Your rights

  • See and delete: saved places and search history, in the app; temporary contacts with the “Delete the contacts G Navigation made” button.
  • Withdraw consent: turn contributions or speed sharing off in the app; turn off location, microphone, camera, contacts, notification or Bluetooth access in Android settings. Navigation keeps working when you decline optional permissions.
  • Delete: individual saved places, or your whole account (section 6).
  • Complaints: contact us as in section 9, or as the personal data protection law provides.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. Your account and sign-in sessions are deleted, the signs and cameras you sent are kept anonymously and no longer linked to you, and your notes and name are taken off incidents. No longer have the app installed? Email support@gnavigation.men with the subject “G Navigation account deletion” and the email you sign in with; we may verify that you own the account and handle it within 30 days. Deleting the account does not reset trial eligibility on the same device.

7. Security

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Service keys live on our servers, not in the app, and each account has only one active sign-in. We access personal data only to handle your requests or moderate reports. No way of sending or storing data is perfectly secure; if an incident affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

8. Children

G Navigation is for people driving or riding and is not directed at anyone under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and know your child created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
